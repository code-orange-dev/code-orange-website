Bitcoin needs builders and teachers.

The developer track turns serious students into upstream contributors. The educator track funds the people who run cohorts, host workshops, and multiply technical Bitcoin education in their own communities.

How It Works

What happens after you apply.

We confirm the project and mentor first. Fellows check in weekly. At Month 3, we look at the work and decide whether to continue, adjust the plan, or pause.

Before applying

Show contributor readiness.

Developers choose a Bitcoin FOSS project and bring useful evidence: code, review, testing, issue investigation, documentation, or a clear technical walkthrough. Educators define what they will teach, who they will serve, and what proof of learning they will collect.

Application

Send a proposal.

Use the GitHub template, include your GitHub or teaching evidence, explain the project target, and show a realistic six-month plan.

Month 1

Onboard with a mentor.

Finalize scope, set weekly check-ins, prepare the environment, and ship the first reproducible artifact.

Months 2-5

Contribute in public.

Every week creates evidence: code, reviews, tests, issue investigation, benchmarks, technical discussion, lesson material, participant progress, or a documented blocker with a next action.

Month 6

Graduate into independence.

Publish the portfolio, present the work, request maintainer references, and prepare applications for OpenSats, HRF, Spiral, employers, or project-specific funding.

Selection Rubric

What makes a strong application.

Seats depend on mentor capacity and project fit. Strong applicants show past work, choose a realistic scope, and communicate well. Developer fellows start with a Contributor Plan. We look at evidence and upstream feedback, not PR quotas.

30 pts

Contribution evidence

Linked PRs, reviews, issues, tests, code reading, teaching logs, or a clear walkthrough of the target project.

25 pts

Project fit and public value

Priority goes to privacy, wallet, node, Lightning, and other infrastructure work with a concrete user or protocol need.

20 pts

Six-month scope

Milestones should fit upstream feedback loops. Small, reviewable, useful work beats ambitious fog.

15 pts

Mentor and maintainer fit

A seat only opens when mentor capacity and project fit are real. Endorsements help, but the work plan matters most.

10 pts

Regional impact

Peer support, workshop help, documentation, or a credible Southeast Asia knowledge-sharing plan.

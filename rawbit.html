/How_It_Works_

Built like a contribution pipeline.

01 Weekly live session Protocol deep-dives on Discord, every week, recorded. Bring questions - sessions are working sessions, not lectures.
02 Homework earns sats ⚡ Each week ships a hands-on assignment against a real node. Complete it, get it reviewed, get paid in sats.
03 Mentor code review Working Bitcoin developers review your code the way maintainers will - so your first real PR isn't your first review.
04 Graduate into OSS Weeks 9–10 point at your first contribution: finding an issue, writing the PR, handling review. Graduation = shipped.

/AI_For_Freedom_

Open intelligence. Private money.

Code Orange workshops explore how privacy-preserving AI, open systems, and Bitcoin can give people more control over communication, work, and money—without treating a platform or institution as the gatekeeper.

Where authorities or platforms censor speech, surveil communities, or restrict financial access, resilient tools can help people communicate, learn, publish, and transact with greater independence. We teach the practical trade-offs, safety boundaries, and responsible use—not hype.

/The_Mission_

Bitcoin needs more builders in Asia.

Southeast Asia is one of the fastest-growing regions for Bitcoin adoption - but it lacks the developers, node runners, and technical educators to support it.

Code Orange was built in Bali to change that. Now headquartered in Singapore, we run hands-on cohorts, weekly workshops, and in-person sessions across the region to turn regular people into sovereign bitcoiners and capable builders.

Bitcoin only. No fluff. Just deep, practical education for people who want to strengthen the network.
